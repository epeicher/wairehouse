Add a "Keep on top" option to desktop windows. It is toggled from a pin button in the window's title bar, next to the existing window controls, and while it is on the window stays above every other window on the desktop, even when another window is focused or opened. Several windows can be kept on top at once; among themselves they stack in normal focus order. Turning it off restores normal stacking. The state is part of the window's session state and survives a page reload. Expose it programmatically as `windowManager.setAlwaysOnTop( windowId, enabled )` and a readable `alwaysOnTop` property on the window. Follow the repository's contributor guide for the workflow.
